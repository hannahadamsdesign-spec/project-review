About

Musician, Mathematician,
Poet, Beekeeper

Lucius Gregory Meredith is a musician, composer, and guitarist whose work moves between structured improvisation, polyrhythmic exploration, and collaborative composition. Performing solo as Leithaus on 8-string touch guitar with loops and spoken word, his music searches for the point where rigor meets feeling.

A student of Robert Fripp's Guitar Craft discipline, a graduate of Oberlin College and the University of Edinburgh, Greg brings a mathematician's ear for pattern and a composer's instinct for surprise.

As a founding member of the musical collective The Crown, he works alongside vocalist Beth Quist and guitarist Tony Geballe, building improvised architectures from voice, strings, and silence.

Beyond music, Greg is the inventor of the rho-calculus, founder of F1R3FLY.io, a published poet, a Tai Chi practitioner, a beekeeper, and human to Fiber the Australian Shepherd. He lives in West Seattle overlooking the Vashon Ferry.

The intersection of music and mathematics isn't metaphor. It's the same activity, heard from different rooms.
