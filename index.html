About

Lucius is Latin for light. Gregory is vigilant in the original Greek. Meredith comes from the Welsh for watcher of the sea. The vigilant light watcher of the sea is the lighthouse. Greg has played with that etymology for decades. In the blockchain world he is known as leithaus, and as a solo musician he records and performs under the name Leithaus.

He has played guitar for nearly forty years and studied within Robert Fripp's Guitar Craft community for fifteen. From 1999 to 2002 he worked with the Senegalese Griots Mapathe and Thione Diop, both in Senegal and in the Pacific Northwest. His current solo work centers on the Kelstone, an 8-string touch guitar designed by Jan Van Kelst, layered with loops and spoken word.

He is a founding member of The Crown, a Seattle-based collective whose recordings move through structured improvisation, rule-based composition, and a wide instrumental vocabulary that includes voice, dulcimer, oud, frame drum, and electric guitar. Personnel has rotated across the years; founding members and frequent collaborators include vocalist Beth Quist and guitarist Tony Geballe. Their full-length album The Path of Totality was released in 2018.

Outside music, Greg is a mathematician and computer scientist. He is the inventor of the rho-calculus, a formal language for distributed concurrent computation, and the founder of F1R3FLY.io. Earlier work includes architecting Microsoft's BizTalk Process Orchestration and the Rosette programming language. His first book of poems, Silence and Surrender, was published by Pomera Press in 2024.

He lives in West Seattle, on a hill that looks down at the Vashon Ferry coming and going.
